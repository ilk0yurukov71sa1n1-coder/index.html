Soft until

Scroll gently and the fibers flow with you. Scroll hard, or swipe fast across the screen, and they rip. Give them a moment and they knit back together.

01 · Flow

Silk that follows you

Every strand bends with the speed of your scroll, so slow movement feels like brushing through fabric.

02 · Tension

Watch the meter

The bar at the bottom shows how hard you are pulling. Cross the line and the fibers snap where they are tightest.

03 · Release

They heal

Torn ends curl back, fray, then reach for each other and join again, ready for the next pull.
