Acceptable use policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

This policy is part of the Terms of service. It keeps Shield safe and reliable for everyone.

You may not use Shield to

  • Break the law, or the terms or usage policies of the AI providers you connect.
  • Use AI provider keys that are not yours or that you are not allowed to use.
  • Resell or share access to the gateway or to someone else's provider keys without our written agreement.
  • Send malware, spam, or content that sexually exploits children.
  • Harass, defraud or impersonate others.

You may not

  • Try to get around plan limits, rate limits, caps or filters, for example by opening several free accounts.
  • Attack, overload, probe or scan the Service, or try to reach data that is not yours.
  • Reverse engineer the Service, except where the law allows it.
  • Share your Rollcfluence keys publicly or in code anyone can read.

Security research

If you find a weakness, please tell us at rollcfluence@gmail.com before telling anyone else, and give us reasonable time to fix it. Testing that stays within your own account and does not harm others is welcome.

What happens if this policy is broken

We may block requests, revoke keys or suspend an account. Where we reasonably can, we tell you first and give you a chance to fix the problem.
