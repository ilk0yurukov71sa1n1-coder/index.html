Security

How we protect this site and your keys

This website

  • HTTPS everywhere with HSTS preload.
  • A strict Content Security Policy: only our own scripts run, and the site cannot be framed.
  • Forms accept same-origin JSON only, are rate limited per IP and include bot traps.
  • No secrets ship to the browser. Every build is scanned for leaked keys before it can deploy.
  • Database tables are locked with row-level security. Only our server can read them.

The gateway

  • Provider keys are encrypted at rest and never returned by the API.
  • Your app holds a scoped Rollcfluence key with its own spend cap, rate limit and allowed models.
  • Keys can be rotated or revoked instantly.
  • Spend caps count requests that are still running, so many requests sent at once cannot slip past a cap.
  • Unknown or premium models are priced at the highest known rate, so a cap is never under-counted.
  • Custom provider and webhook addresses must resolve to the public internet; private and internal networks are refused.

Your account

  • No passwords to steal: you sign in with a link sent to your email, and each link works only once.
  • Opening a sign-in link shows which account it is for and waits for your click, so a link someone else sends you cannot sign you in to their account.
  • Signing out ends your session on every device.
  • Session cookies are HttpOnly and Secure, so page scripts can never read them.

Report a vulnerability

Found something? See our security.txt or email rollcfluence@gmail.com with the subject "Security report". Please give us a chance to fix it before sharing it publicly. We reply to every report.
