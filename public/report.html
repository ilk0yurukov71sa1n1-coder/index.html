3. A worked example

An illustration, not a measurement. A key leaks on a Friday evening and nobody looks until Monday morning, about 2.5 days.

Without a cap, at Sysdig's worst-case rate:
2.5 days × $46,000 a day ≈ $115,000

With a Shield spend cap you set at, say, $50 a day:
2.5 days × $50 a day = $125 at most

The real figure depends on the attacker and the models they use. $82,314 run up in about two days on a stolen Google Cloud key used for Gemini. The startup normally spent about $180 a month. (The Register, March 2026 ↗). For comparison, the Shield Pro plan is €49 a month.

4. Proven ways to cut the AI bill

Independent research. Shield does not do these for you today; they work alongside it.

Peer-reviewed paper · Chen, Zaharia, Zou · Stanford · TMLR · 2024

FrugalGPT: How to Use Large Language Models While Reducing Cost and Improving Performance

Up to 98% lower cost while matching GPT-4 accuracy, by sending each query to the cheapest model that can answer it well.

Fine print: 98% was the best of three datasets; the other two saved 73% and 59%. Read the source ↗

Peer-reviewed paper · Ong et al. · UC Berkeley, Anyscale, Canva · ICLR · 2025

RouteLLM: Learning to Route LLMs from Preference Data

Over 2x cheaper in some settings without losing response quality, by routing easy prompts to a smaller model.

Fine print: The paper says "in certain cases"; savings depend on your traffic. Read the source ↗

Research preprint · Regmi, Pun · arXiv · 2024

GPT Semantic Cache: Reducing LLM Costs and Latency via Semantic Embedding Caching

Up to 68.8% fewer API calls by answering repeated, similar questions from a cache instead of the model.

Fine print: A preprint, not yet peer-reviewed. Works best when users ask similar things. Read the source ↗

Peer-reviewed paper · Jiang et al. · Microsoft Research · EMNLP · 2023

LLMLingua: Compressing Prompts for Accelerated Inference of Large Language Models

Up to 20x shorter prompts with little loss in quality, so you pay for far fewer input tokens.

Fine print: At 20x Microsoft reports about a 1.5 point drop; results vary by task. Read the source ↗

Provider documentation · Anthropic · 2024

Prompt caching with Claude

Up to 90% lower cost and up to 85% lower latency for long prompts that repeat, such as a big system prompt or document.

Fine print: Only the cached part of the prompt gets cheaper, and writing to the cache costs extra. Read the source ↗

Provider documentation · OpenAI · 2024

Batch API

50% off for requests that can wait: results come back within 24 hours at half the normal price.

Fine print: Not for live chat, and not every model supports it. Read the source ↗

5. What Shield does, and what it does not

  • Does: keeps provider keys in a vault, puts hard spend caps and rate limits on every key, screens prompts for injection, masks personal data, fails over to a backup model and logs every request.
  • Does not today: model routing, response caching or prompt compression (section 4). Those cut the bill; Shield stops the bill from being stolen. They work well together.
  • Prices: Starter €0, Pro €49 a month, Team €299 a month, excluding VAT. Provider usage is billed by the providers.

6. Limits of this report

  • Market forecasts come from analyst firms and differ in scope; the Gartner figure is mostly hardware.
  • Incident costs are reported cases and worst-case estimates. Most leaks are smaller, some are larger.
  • Research savings were measured on the authors' benchmarks. Your traffic will give different results.
