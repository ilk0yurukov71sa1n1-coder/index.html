Privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Rollcfluence Shield ("Shield", "we", "us") collects, why, how long we keep it and what rights you have. It covers this website, the dashboard at /app and the gateway at /v1. It is written to meet the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who is responsible

Rollcfluence is the controller for the data described in this policy. Contact us about anything in it at rollcfluence@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

When your app sends AI requests through the gateway, you are the controller of the content of those requests and we act as your processor. Our Data processing terms cover that part.

2. What we collect

Early access listEmail address, the goal you picked, country (from network headers), whether you confirmed your email, sign-up time.
AccountEmail address, plan, sign-up and last sign-in time.
Gateway keysKey name, settings and limits. We store only a one-way fingerprint of each key, never the key itself.
Provider keys you addStored encrypted and used only to forward your own requests. We show only the last four characters.
Usage recordsFor each request: time, key, provider, model, status, token counts, estimated cost, response time, the city and country it came from, and the end-user id your app sends, if any. We do not store prompt or response content, with one exception you control: if your app turns on response caching for a request, the answer is kept encrypted for up to 7 days so a repeat question can be answered free.
Security logSign-ins, setting changes, blocks and locks. IP addresses are checked in the moment for your IP and country rules and are not stored.
Alert channelsEmail addresses or webhook links you add for alerts. Webhook links are stored encrypted.
BillingPlan, subscription status and renewal date. Card details, billing address and tax ID are collected and held by our payment processor. We never see or store your card number.
Messages to usQuestions from the chat helper and feedback scores or comments, with your email if you give it and your country.
Rate limitingA short-lived counter per IP address or key, which expires within minutes.

3. Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • To provide the service (accounts, keys, the gateway, limits, alerts, billing): performance of our contract with you, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b).
  • To keep the service and your keys secure (rate limits, security log, abuse prevention): our legitimate interest in protecting the service and our customers, Art. 6(1)(f).
  • To invite you from the early access list and to answer your questions: your request and our legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(b) and (f).
  • To keep invoices and tax records: legal obligation, Art. 6(1)(c).

We do not sell personal data, we do not use it for advertising and we do not make automated decisions with legal effect about you. Blocks that Shield applies to requests follow rules you set or obvious abuse patterns, and you can review and undo them in your dashboard.

4. Cookies

We set one cookie, and only when you sign in to the dashboard: a session cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It is strictly necessary, so it needs no consent banner. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and no third-party scripts.

5. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers, each bound by a data processing agreement and only allowed to use the data to provide their service to us:

  • Cloud hosting and serverless compute.
  • A managed database, hosted in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • A short-lived counter store for rate limiting.
  • An email delivery service, for sign-in links and alerts.
  • A payment processor, for subscriptions, invoices and tax.

The current list of these providers is available on request at rollcfluence@gmail.com. When you use the gateway, your requests go to the AI provider you chose (for example OpenAI or Anthropic) under your own account with them, and their privacy terms apply to that content.

6. Transfers outside the EU

Our database is in the EU. Some of our providers process data in the United States, including the servers that run the gateway. Where that happens we rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, with extra safeguards such as encryption in transit and at rest.

7. How long we keep it

  • Usage records: 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Account, keys, alert channels and security log: while your account is open, then deleted within 30 days of closing it.
  • Early access list: until you are invited, ask us to remove you, or early access ends.
  • Questions and feedback: until answered and no longer needed, at most 12 months.
  • Billing records: as long as tax law requires, usually up to 10 years.
  • Rate-limit counters: minutes.

8. How we protect it

All traffic is encrypted with TLS. Provider keys and webhook links are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Gateway keys are stored only as fingerprints. Database tables are locked so only our server can read them. More detail is on our Security page.

9. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to withdraw any consent you gave. Email rollcfluence@gmail.com. You can also delete keys, provider keys and alert channels yourself in the dashboard. You have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Children

Shield is a service for businesses and developers. It is not meant for anyone under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell account holders by email before the change takes effect.
