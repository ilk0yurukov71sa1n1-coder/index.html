Privacy

Last updated 16 October 2026 · Early access

This page explains what this website collects and why. Rollcfluence Shield is in early access, so the list is short.

What we collect

  • Early access signups: your email address, the country your request came from (from our host's network headers) and the time you signed up.
  • Questions from the chat helper: the question you typed, your email address and your country, so we can answer you.
  • We do not use advertising cookies or trackers on this site.

Why

To send you your Rollcfluence key when your batch opens, and to reply to questions you ask us. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Where it is stored

The site is hosted on Vercel. Signups and questions are stored in a Supabase database that only our server can read. Rate limiting uses Upstash and keeps only a short-lived counter per IP address, which expires after a minute.

How long

We keep signups until you ask us to delete them or early access ends, and questions until they are answered and no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time. Email rollcfluence@gmail.com and we will act on it within 30 days.
