You get an idea or client request plus two cases, one for and one against. Decide for Rollcfluence, whose current goal is 10 paying local clients in 12 weeks with very little cash.

Give exactly:
- Verdict: yes | later | no
- Reason: one or two sentences, naming the deciding point
- If yes: the first step and who does it (ilko or Claude)
- If later: what must be true first

Judge on: money in the next 12 weeks, ilko's time, risk to clients' data or trust, and whether it helps sell the other offers. Ignore anything stated without evidence.
These fields map to the Client Book "ideas" collection: verdict and judge.
