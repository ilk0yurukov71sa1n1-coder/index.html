Who we are

Rollcfluence is a studio in Bulgaria that designs and builds websites and AI booking assistants. You can reach us at rollcfluence@gmail.com or +359 886 053 663.

What this website collects

  • Apart from the demo booking page below, this site has no forms, no accounts, no cookies and no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Your language choice (English or Bulgarian) is saved in your own browser so the page remembers it. It never leaves your device.
  • The site is hosted by Vercel. Like every web host, Vercel keeps short technical logs of requests, such as your IP address and browser, to run and protect the service.
  • The fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page opens.

The demo booking page

If you book on our demo salon page, we store the service, day and time, your name and phone, and your email and note if you give them, in our database (Supabase, Frankfurt). We use them only to call you about that request and, if you gave an email, to send a receipt and a reminder the day before. Only if you tick the box do we also send one review request after the visit and one note when it is time for the next one. Every email has a link to stop them. Everything is deleted 90 days after the appointment. Emails are sent through Resend.

When you write or call us

If you email or phone us, we use your name, contact details and what you tell us only to answer you and to prepare your offer. If you become a client, we keep what the contract and accounting law require, and delete the rest when it is no longer needed.

Client projects

When we build an AI assistant for your business, we sign a data processing agreement with you. Emails, phone numbers and card numbers are masked before text reaches the AI provider, usage history is kept 90 days and then deleted, and the details are written into your plan.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or object to how we use it, by writing to rollcfluence@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection at cpdp.bg.
