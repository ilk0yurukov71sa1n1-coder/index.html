Questions

Before you call.

How long does it take?

A page takes 1 to 3 working days once we have your texts and photos. An assistant takes about 5 working days to build and test once we have your details, so most go live about 2 weeks after the first call.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo, 5 to 15 photos, your services and prices, your address and opening hours, and who should receive the enquiries.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. We set up everything, and you only need a phone to see the result.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, and if you leave you get all the files.

Can I cancel the monthly fee?

Yes, with 30 days’ notice. Your page goes offline at the end of the period, and you keep the domain and files.

Is it GDPR safe?

For assistants we sign a data processing agreement with you, and personal data is masked before it reaches the AI provider.

What if the assistant doesn’t know the answer?

It says so and passes the conversation to you, instead of guessing.
