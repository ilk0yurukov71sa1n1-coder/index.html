What others don’t show you

What the price really includes, written down.

We read the public pages of 13 chatbot sellers in Bulgaria and Romania. This is what most of them leave out, and exactly what we do instead.

  1. 01

    A ceiling on the AI bill

    What we found

    None of the 13 publishes a limit on what the AI itself can cost you.

    What we do

    Every assistant has a daily and a monthly spend cap. When a cap is reached, the assistant pauses instead of the bill growing.

  2. 02

    Prices before the call

    What we found

    6 of the 13 show no price at all.

    What we do

    “From” prices on this page, and a fixed price in your one-page plan before any work starts.

  3. 03

    Customer data, in plain words

    What we found

    We found no public page that says what happens to customer details before they reach the AI.

    What we do

    Emails, phone numbers and card numbers are replaced with [email], [phone] and [card] before the text leaves Shield. Names are not masked, and we tell you so.

  4. 04

    Tricks and floods stopped

    What we found

    Rarely mentioned.

    What we do

    Well-known tricks like “ignore your instructions” are refused, each visitor has a per-minute limit, and one abusive visitor is paused for 10 minutes while everyone else keeps chatting. The filter catches common phrasings, not every attack.

  5. 05

    A stolen key is useless

    What we found

    Rarely mentioned.

    What we do

    The assistant’s key can be tied to your website’s servers, blocked by country, set to expire and locked in one click. You get an email alert, and every change is logged.

  6. 06

    Repeat questions cost nothing

    What we found

    Rarely mentioned.

    What we do

    When the same question comes in again, Shield can answer it from memory, so the AI provider isn’t paid twice.

  7. 07

    Proof every month

    What we found

    Reports are rarely part of the price.

    What we do

    A short monthly report: how many questions the assistant answered, how many booking requests it passed on, what the AI cost and what Shield blocked.

  8. 08

    Not tied to one AI company

    What we found

    Most bots run on a single provider.

    What we do

    Shield works with 10 AI providers, including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini and Mistral. We can switch the model without rebuilding your assistant.

  9. 09

    You own it

    What we found

    Ownership terms are rarely published.

    What we do

    The domain is registered in your name, and if you ever leave you get all your files.

Questions

Before you call.

How long does it take?

A page takes 1 to 3 working days once we have your texts and photos. An assistant takes about 5 working days to build and test once we have your details, so most go live about 2 weeks after the first call.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo, 5 to 15 photos, your services and prices, your address and opening hours, and who should receive the enquiries.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. We set up everything, and you only need a phone to see the result.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, and if you leave you get all the files.

Can I cancel the monthly fee?

Yes, with 30 days’ notice. Your page goes offline at the end of the period, and you keep the domain and files.

Is it GDPR safe?

For assistants we sign a data processing agreement with you, and personal data is masked before it reaches the AI provider.

What if the assistant doesn’t know the answer?

It says so and passes the conversation to you, instead of guessing.
